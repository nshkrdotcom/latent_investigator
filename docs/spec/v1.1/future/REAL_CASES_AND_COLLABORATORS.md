# Future Extension — Resolved Real Cases and Collaborators

The initial synthetic experiment gives exact hidden truth but limited realism. Later validation should add cases not authored by the system designer.

## Candidate domains

- SRE / production incidents
- security investigations
- OSINT investigations
- transportation/accident investigations
- investigative journalism cases where enough of the record later became known

## Standard case packet

Ask a domain collaborator to provide:

### Visible packet

What an investigator actually knew at the selected starting time.

### Evidence timeline

When additional facts became available.

### Resolution packet

What the domain owner now believes happened, which evidence drove that conclusion, and what remains uncertain.

### Hidden grading notes

Material facts needed to evaluate the investigator but not shown initially.

## Why collaborators matter

The same person designing simulator, ontology, evaluator, and reference solver creates co-adaptation risk. A few independently authored cases can be more informative than thousands of self-generated variations.

## Claim discipline

Resolved real cases provide retrospective external anchors. They do not become perfect causal ground truth merely because an expert signed off on the resolution.
