# Experiment Overview

The experimental program is split into gates and includes an even cheaper **baseline reconnaissance** before the structured core is complete.

## Baseline reconnaissance — is there headroom at all?

Once the simulator exists, immediately run:

- B0a strong monolithic frontier baseline;
- B0b compute-matched iterative monolithic baseline.

Sweep from smoke/small toward medium/large as budget permits. If B0b is already near ceiling at the largest intended feasible scale, pause before building the rest of the structured architecture.

## Gate 0 — executable truth

Before structured model comparisons, prove that simulator, oracle evaluator, aggregation, provenance, parsimony fixtures, resolution classes, and replay behave correctly.

## Gate 1 — does structure add value?

Compare B0a, B0b, and B1 structured frontier investigator.

Gate 1 cannot pass merely because deterministic provenance bookkeeping works. Its co-primary outcomes are:

1. final access-relative material conclusion quality;
2. true-world retention;
3. recovery after planted disconfirmation.

Resolution balance is a guardrail so always-hedge and always-resolve policies both fail.

If Gate 1 shows no repeatable advantage at the predeclared relevant scale, stop before evaluator optimization.

## Gate 2 — evaluator substitution

Holding generator, attack prompts, simulator seeds, aggregation, search policy, and evidence budget fixed, compare local evaluators:

- oracle;
- frontier general model;
- cheap general model;
- System One/Jev.

Measure local agreement, final investigation quality, cost, latency, and achievable search breadth under fixed budgets.

## Generation-bottleneck diagnostic

Separately compare actual candidate generation against a generation-oracle arm where the hidden true world is injected into the candidate set. This reveals whether local judgment is being optimized around a generator that simply fails to propose the right mechanism.

## Access-curve extension

After the first gates, rerun the same hidden world under public/limited/operator/oracle evidence regimes. This tests whether conclusions become more decisive only when access actually resolves material distinctions.
